# What the market asks for, October 6, 2026

## Short version

- Explicit fine-tuning and open-model requirements show up at inference-platform and GPU companies (Together AI, NVIDIA, CoreWeave, Fireworks). They are absent from the model-lab applied-AI postings that were read (Anthropic), which ask for evals, agents, MCP and prompt skills.
- Several of the strongest "demand" postings are already closed. Live evidence rests mainly on Together AI, CoreWeave and Databricks postings updated in late September and early October 2026.
- Demand for open models is growing on cost grounds (a September 2026 survey says 34 percent of enterprise token usage), while an older survey put open models at 11 percent of enterprise spend. Both can be true.
- Evidence for paid demand for private or customized models comes mostly from vendor marketing. Treat prices as directional.
- Hiring-advice sources disagree on whether a bare fine-tuned model helps a portfolio. A measured comparison (eval, cost and latency) is the safer artifact.

Confidence words: `verified`, `likely`, `not verified`. See the [README](README.md). These are general facts about public postings and public surveys. Each source is shown as ([label](link), date).

## 4. What portfolio evidence is valued

The sources disagree, and both are opinion or vendor analysis, not hiring-manager data:

- A DEV article (2026-03-07) argues that "a fine-tuned model on Hugging Face" is something every candidate's portfolio already has. `likely` ([DEV](https://dev.to/klement_gunndu/5-ai-portfolio-projects-that-actually-get-you-hired-in-2026-5bpl), 2026-03-07).
- A posting analysis (jobsbyculture, 2026-05, "1,800+ postings across 116 companies") calls fine-tuning together with inference optimization "the single highest-signal skill in 2026" ($220K to $350K), with evals prominent at senior levels. The method is not described. The researcher's figure of 1,886 roles was not seen. `likely` ([jobsbyculture](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/top-ai-ml-skills-employers-hire-for-2026), 2026-05).
- Taken together, the safest artifact is an end-to-end project: data, a held-out eval against a prompt-only baseline and a frontier API, cost and latency numbers, the tuned adapter, a model card and a write-up. This is the briefs' reading of the evidence, not a sourced finding. `likely`.
- Certifications carry weak signal. NVIDIA's NCA-GENL costs $125, takes 1 hour, has 50 to 60 questions and is valid 2 years (`verified` from [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/learn/certification/generative-ai-llm-associate/), 2026). A vendor-blog survey claims only 23 percent of managers screen for AI certifications; that figure was not rechecked and the method is unknown. `not verified` ([rework.com](https://resources.rework.com/news/ai-jobs-skills/ai-certification-market-2026-what-matters), 2026). No certification was found cited in the postings read.

## 5. Gaps

- Primary Fireworks, OpenAI, Mistral and Hugging Face postings could not be read.

- No 2026 survey from Menlo or similar with a fresh open-weight share, apart from the one ETR survey.
- Upwork and freelance-rate pages could not be read.
- Legal and political risk for Chinese open weights was researched only lightly (see [landscape.md](landscape.md)).
