# The open-weight model landscape, October 6, 2026

## Short version

- Chinese labs hold the open-weight lead. On one index (Artificial Analysis v4.3.2, read October 6, 2026) the best Chinese open model scores 46 and the best US open model scores about 25.
- US labs have shipped real open models since August 2025 (gpt-oss, Gemma 4, Nemotron 3, Inkling, Muse Glimmer), but none has closed the gap on capability or downloads.
- Open models trail closed ones by roughly 4 months overall (Epoch AI). Other evaluators give very different numbers by domain.
- Many of the newest big releases use custom licenses with revenue or hosting limits. Apache-2.0 and MIT are the safe defaults. Read each license before client work.
- Download weights only from verified organization accounts. Impostor and "uncensored" copies of popular models exist.

Confidence words: `verified`, `likely`, `not verified`. See the [README](README.md) for what they mean. Each source is shown as ([label](link), date).

## 4. Usage and download data

- **Hugging Face Summer 2026 report (Aug 14, 2026):** the largest Chinese open model each month of 2026 exceeded any US release. Chinese ceilings ran from 754B to 2.78T parameters. US models stayed under about 130B in most months, except Nemotron 3 Ultra. Models under 1B parameters account for 83 percent of all-time downloads. `verified` ([report](https://huggingface.co/blog/state-of-open-models-summer-2026), 2026-08-14).
- **Qwen's totals conflict between two readings of the same report.** The report itself, as read by the models researcher, gives 151,448 derivatives and 2.045B downloads. Fortune, citing the same report, gives about 3 billion downloads in the preceding six months (against Google 418M and Meta 227M) and over 300,000 derivatives. These may measure different periods or scopes. `verified` for the Hugging Face report's 151,448 derivatives and 2.045B downloads; `likely` for Fortune's figures; how the two relate was not resolved ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/15/alibaba-qwen-open-ai-models-3-billion-downloads-meta-google/), 2026-08-15).
- **Top repositories, Sep 18, 2026:** Qwen holds 27 of the top 60 repositories (36 counting repacks, 240.1M downloads in 30 days). Gemma is the number-two family with 32.6M downloads. A "rank 8" figure that circulated is a single Gemma repository, not a family rank. `verified` ([DigitalApplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/most-downloaded-open-ai-models-hugging-face-september-2026), 2026-09-18).
- **ATOM Project:** China and the US were at parity around 300M downloads each in Aug 2025, with China growing faster. A claim that China had 41 percent of downloads is `not verified` ([ATOM](https://www.atomproject.ai/), 2026-03-29).
- **OpenRouter token shares measure different things.** Treat none of these as "the" share. `verified` that they differ in definition:
  - 61 percent: Chinese share of tokens among the top 10 models in the week of Feb 24, 2026 ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/02/25/chinese-ai-models-hit-61-market-share-on-openrouter/), 2026-02-25).
  - US labs fell from about 70 to about 30 percent of all tokens between Jun 2025 and Jun 2026 ([OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/share-of-us-models-being-used-on-openrouter-has-collapsed-from-70-to-30-over-the-past-year/), 2026-06-26).
  - Over 80 percent: Chinese share of open-model tokens only (Lambert). Mozilla's 46 percent is the peak Chinese share of routed tokens.
  - Late 2025 baseline: open models about one third of usage; Chinese open models rose from a 1.2 percent low in late 2024 to nearly 30 percent of all usage in some weeks. The 13 percent figure that circulated is the one-year average weekly share, not the endpoint ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/state-of-ai), 2025-12).
- **Developer surveys:** Mozilla reports 79 percent of professional developers use open models and 71 percent use closed (n=954). `verified` ([Mozilla](https://stateofopensource.ai/), 2026-09).
- **Enterprise use.** Two surveys, measuring different things:
  - A September 2026 survey by Enterprise Technology Research (n=200) puts open-weight models at 34 percent of enterprise token usage, up from 23 a year earlier and expected to reach 41 percent. 42 percent of respondents had open-weight models in production (31 percent in July), and 69 percent of those cited cost. `likely` ([Techstrong, Sep 22, 2026](https://techstrong.ai/articles/open-weight-ai-models-expected-to-capture-41-of-enterprise-token-usage/), 2026-09-22).
  - Menlo Ventures (Dec 2025) put open-source LLMs at 11 percent of enterprise usage, down from 19 percent a year earlier. Menlo has published no 2026 update. `verified` ([Menlo](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/), 2025-12-09).
  - The first is a share of tokens and the second a share of spend, so both can be true.
- **Cost.** Benaich (May 2026): none of the Chinese open coding models costs more than a third of Claude Opus 4.7. A developer reported about $10 for an hour of coding on Claude versus under 50 cents on DeepSeek. `verified` as quotes ([State of AI, May 2026](https://nathanbenaich.substack.com/p/state-of-ai-may-2026), 2026-05; [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2026/when-americans-choose-chinese-ai/), 2026-06-17).

Lawmakers opened investigations. `verified` ([Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2026/when-americans-choose-chinese-ai/), 2026-06-17). ## 5. Policy and provenance risk

- **NSA, CISA and FBI advisory AA26-251A (Sep 8, 2026)** names six Chinese labs (DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun, Z.AI) and alleges industrial-scale distillation of US models. Its three recommendations are aimed at US AI companies: detect distillation, subtly alter responses, share intelligence. It does not tell users to vet open-weight provenance. That advice comes from the Cloud Security Alliance's own summary and should not be credited to the government. `verified` ([CISA advisory](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-251a), 2026-09-08; [CSA note](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-china-ai-distillation-campaign-20260918-cs/), 2026-09-18).
- **Sanctions:** none found against the named labs through early October 2026. Checked: OFAC recent actions (Sep 18 to Oct 5, first page only), Treasury releases (to Oct 6), BIS news (July to Oct 2) and a Federal Register search from July 1. A Treasury official said on Jul 21 that sanctions on distilling models were possible within "days or weeks" (secondary reports). `verified` for no action found ([OFAC](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions), 2026-10-06; [Vibranium Labs](https://vibraniumlabs.ai/blog/chinese-ai-models-sanctioned-whats-actually-happening), 2026-07-22). Allegations that Moonshot reached banned chips are secondary only and `not verified`.
- **Z.ai (Zhipu) has been on the US Commerce Entity List since Jan 16, 2025.** That restricts US exports to the company. It does not by itself bar US users from downloading GLM weights, but it matters for paying or contracting with Z.ai. Zhipu's presence in that rule was inferred from a Federal Register search match. `verified` ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/16/2025-00704/addition-of-entities-to-and-revision-of-entry-on-the-entity-list), 2025-01-16).
- **Narrow restrictions so far.** Several states ban DeepSeek on state devices (2025). A bipartisan "No Adversarial AI Act" would bar federal agencies from using listed adversarial AI, but its 2026 status is unknown. A claim that GitHub Copilot turns Kimi K2.7 Code off by default is `not verified`. `likely` ([GovTech](https://www.govtech.com/biz/data/wheres-deepseek-banned-the-states-blocking-chinese-made-ai), 2025).

## 6. Open-weight is not open-source

The Open Source Initiative's Open Source AI Definition 1.0 requires data information, training and inference code, and parameters under approved terms. Most "open" language models fail it. Llama and most custom-license models are source-available. You can run and fine-tune the weights but generally cannot reproduce them, and the legal status of a fine-tune depends on the base license. Ai2's Olmo is the usual "fully open" example, though small and not frontier. Mozilla also finds no open release ships the full data recipe. Not legal advice. `verified` ([OSI definition](https://opensource.org/ai/open-source-ai-definition), 2024-10; [Mozilla](https://stateofopensource.ai/), 2026-09).

## 8. Download only from verified organization accounts

- Impostor and modified copies are common. The Muse Glimmer repository that one researcher first cited (`meta-muse`) belongs to an unverified organization with one member and three followers, created Oct 1, 2026, and it also hosts an "uncensored" copy. The official repository is `meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B`, verified as Meta Inc. and created Aug 9, 2026. Many abliterated or "heretic" GGUF copies also exist. `verified` ([impostor org](https://huggingface.co/api/organizations/meta-muse/overview), 2026-10-06; [official org](https://huggingface.co/api/organizations/meta-models/overview), 2026-10-06; [search](https://huggingface.co/api/models?search=Muse-Glimmer&sort=downloads&limit=20), 2026-10-06).
- Practical rule: for client work, take weights only from the lab's own verified organization or from long-standing verified conversion organizations (the verifier named `unsloth`, `mlx-community` and `lmstudio-community`). Before downloading, check the verified badge, the member count and the repository creation date, and confirm the official repository name from the lab's own page.
- Hugging Face's own page for a model can show a wrong date. Use the license file and the organization's listing, not only the card header.
